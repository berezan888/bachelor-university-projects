- Project Concept: Smart Meal Planner — conceptual design, financial budgeting, and project management plan for an automated food inventory and meal planning mobile app.

## 📚 About the Course

This folder contains the materials and the group semester project for the Manažment projektov (Project Management) course at TUKE (FEI).

- Goal: Understand project management methodologies, master planning and scheduling tools, and apply them through a comprehensive semester project lifecycle.

## 📱 Project Concept: Smart Meal Planner App

Our team developed a complete project management and financial plan for the **Smart Meal Planner** — an innovative mobile application designed to optimize meal planning, reduce food waste, and automate grocery lists using modern technologies.

### Key Deliverables & Analysis:

1. **Project Initiation & Strategic Analysis:** Evaluated project scope, defined success criteria, and conducted a comprehensive SWOT analysis to identify application strengths, technical limitations, and market opportunities.
2. **Stakeholder & Resource Management:** Built a detailed stakeholder matrix covering internal team members (PM, developers, QA, legal) and external entities (investors like *Rock Health, Fitt Capital*, OCR and cloud technology suppliers).
3. **Financial & Cost Planning:** Estimated total project development costs (€112,586 – €139,142) across a 3–6 month lifecycle, including personnel salaries, software licenses, infrastructure, and targeted marketing campaigns (€21,600).
4. **Execution, Scheduling & Risk Assessment:** Developed a Work Breakdown Structure (WBS) for task decomposition, analyzed critical project risks (technical glitches, data privacy, market competition) with mitigation strategies, and prepared timeline frameworks.
