## 📱 Project Concept: MAU App

Our team developed a complete business and analytical product concept for **MAU** — an innovative mobile application designed to solve accuracy and feature-limitations found in existing period trackers.

### Key Deliverables & Analysis:
1. **Problem Definition & Market Research:** Analyzed current market alternatives (*Flo, Clue, My Calendar*) and identified gaps in quality and data precision.
2. **Analytical Methods:** Applied **Value Analysis** and **SWOT Analysis** to evaluate solutions and choose the best strategic benchmark.
3. **Strategic Management:** Developed a **Balanced Scorecard (BSC)** across financial, customer, internal, and growth perspectives, alongside a **Business Model Canvas (BMC)** for target customer segments (women aged 12–55).
4. **Final Proposal:** Formulated the unique value proposition of MAU, integrating AI-driven predictions, symptom-focused monitoring, and a built-in community concept.
